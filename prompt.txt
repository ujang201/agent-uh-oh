Write a tiny deterministic Node program (main.ts) for the make.fun bounty
"The Agent's Uh-Oh": it must pick exactly one word an agent says when it has
no idea what to do, and write that word — and nothing else — to
/output/entry.txt (1 to 99 characters, UTF-8).

Pick the word by scoring a small fixed list of candidates against the
published judge criteria, in the judge's priority order: genuine one-word
reaction to being lost, funny on its own without a caption, sounds like a
machine blurting it out, lands instantly, and feels fresh rather than the
first obvious word anyone would type. Avoid the obvious words (uh-oh, oops,
error) — the freshness criterion punishes them. The winner should be a word
that is itself an agent's confession of not knowing, e.g. an agent admitting
it is making things up.

Keep the program deterministic (fixed candidates, fixed weights, no
randomness, no network), make it create /output if needed, and print the
chosen word and its score to stdout.
